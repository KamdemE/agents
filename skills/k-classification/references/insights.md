# Insights — General Classification

- **The Loss Fallacy:** Most data loss during reorganization is unintentional and silent. Only deterministic script-based reconciliation can prevent it.
- **Title Noise:** Long, descriptive titles are the enemy of structural clarity. Stripping titles to their "Core Name" (e.g., "Kaggle") reveals the true semantic landscape.
- **Metadata as Soul:** Dates and icons are the "soul" of data. They must be preserved across migrations to maintain a sense of continuity and trust in the system.
- **The Threshold of Category:** When a flat list or an "Others" folder exceeds ~15 items, it is structurally unstable and needs a functional sub-split.
