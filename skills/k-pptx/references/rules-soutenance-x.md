# École polytechnique oral defence — rules digest

Source: **Directive de soutenance du stage en entreprise**,
n° 53339/EP/DFHM/PROMO, 5 May 2026, promotion X2024 — Annexe 3 "Méthodologie
pour la soutenance orale" and Annexe 4 "Grille d'évaluation"; plus the
**Attendus Stage 2A** briefing. Read in full on 23 August 2026 for
`assets/presentation_x_v2/`.

**Verify against the current year's directive before reusing.** Rules change
between promotions — the 2026 text explicitly annuls its predecessors, and one
rule reversed from the previous year (see †).

## Timing

| Phase | Duration |
|---|---|
| presentation | 15 min — past the 16th minute the jury may cut you off |
| questions | 10 min |
| deliberation, in camera | 5 min |
| debriefing, with the student | 5 min |

Dress: "tenue Forum" (suit). Be ready 5–10 minutes early. Bring a laptop with
HDMI **and** a USB copy. Test the projector beforehand.

## The deck

- **A dozen useful slides** at most for 15 minutes.
- **Slide 1** follows the written report's cover rules: student name, internship
  dates, title, host entity **and its location**. An illustration is allowed.
  The **École logo must be the most visible** — no confusion about provenance.
- **Slide 2** is a sommaire, level-1 titles only, deliberately synthetic — not a
  table of contents. † *"L'indication dans le sommaire des numéros de planche
  des différentes parties est inutile."* (Page references there were expected in
  earlier years; in 2026 they are declared useless.)
- **The development must not restate the report**, which the jury has read. The
  plan **must differ** from the report's, to foreground the analysis of the
  company and the lessons drawn. This is a graded criterion:
  *non-reprise du mémoire*.
- **The conclusion slide** synthesises the main lines and ideally opens onto a
  pertinent question. **It stays on screen for the whole question phase**, so
  its form and content matter more than any other slide's.
- **A "Merci de votre attention" or "Questions" closing slide is forbidden.**
- **Annexes may follow the conclusion** to answer anticipated questions. They do
  not count in the dozen.

## Form

- One graphic line throughout. *"La plupart des entreprises ont leurs propres
  modèles, il est recommandé de les utiliser ou de s'en inspirer."*
- White or very light monochrome ground.
- No tone-on-tone — it becomes illegible under projection.
- Animations generally to be avoided.
- **"L'espace disponible sur la planche doit être utilisé de manière optimale,
  elle ne doit être ni vide, ni surchargée."**
- **"Les planches ne doivent pas comporter de phrases, mais uniquement des idées
  maîtresses. Il est habituel qu'une planche comporte moins de six lignes et
  moins de six mots par ligne."** On arbitrating those two, see
  `typography.md` §density.
- Images welcome without abuse, and only if they carry a message — "le support
  n'est pas un album photographique".
- Pictograms welcome, but must be univocal.
- **Slides must be numbered (x/x).** A reminder of position in the structure on
  each slide "peut être utile", especially with many slides.

## Delivery

Voice: volume, articulation, rhythm, pauses, no verbal tics, no familiar
register. Eyes: **face the jury about 90 % of the time**; avoid looking at the
screen and never turn your back to them. Posture: anchored, no swaying. Hands:
worked, supporting the message.

Answers: concise, reformulate the question to buy a second, be sincere, and say
plainly when you do not know. Do not keep talking after answering.

## What is graded (Annexe 4) — four equal domains

**Written report** — form and style only.

**Oral** — timing, structure, development and analysis, conclusion and opening,
introduction and problem statement, ability to illustrate, *non-reprise du
mémoire*, persuasiveness, distance and objectivity, precision, argument,
**anecdotes**, gesture, posture, delivery, clarity, vocabulary, spontaneity, and
the support itself: **legibility, attractiveness, originality**.

**Lessons about the company** — competitive position and strategy,
organisational analysis, **business model and key figures**, ability to observe
and understand how the company works, distance on the role held.

**Lessons about yourself** — critical thinking, analysis, introspection,
self-analysis of strengths and weaknesses **and their evolution**, the tutor's
appraisal, **ability to project into a professional future**.

Scale per criterion with target quotas: EX 10 %, TB 25 %, B 40 %, AB 25 %, INS
if justified. C minimum validates the year.

## From the Attendus briefing

- *"Évitez qu'elles soient trop ternes. Mais pas trop d'originalité quand
  même."* A sober rich template is exactly the answer.
- The three axes to analyse: the company and its environment (key figures,
  operation, sector and strategy); your own behaviour on the mission plus
  local management; your soft skills, strengths and areas to improve, and your
  appetite for that kind of company.
- Answer explicitly: **how did this internship change my academic and
  professional plans?** Including, failing a positive answer, which sectors do
  *not* suit you.
- *"Parlez vrai ! C'est surtout votre franchise qu'on va évaluer."*
- Annexe 1 adds: *"Il sera porté une attention particulière aux relations
  femmes-hommes au sein de l'entreprise."* Prepare it — and say what your
  observation does **not** allow you to conclude.
