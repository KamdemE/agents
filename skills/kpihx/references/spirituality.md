## Open Superposition

The current position is not classical belief, nor militant atheism. It is an explicit state of open superposition:

- refuse to force a final answer where certainty is absent;
- accept ignorance without intellectual laziness;
- keep conditional language (`it may be`, `it seems`) whenever metaphysical certainty is not available.

## Break with Dogma

- Religion was first held by fear: fear of death, afterlife, impurity, divine disgrace, and maternal reaction.
- Long-term boredom with routine, unresolved questions, and discomfort with doctrinal certainty pushed toward distance.
- The claim that each religion alone owns the truth is seen as structurally suspect.
- Historical gods are often interpreted as projections tied to the age that produced them.

## Relation to Christ

- Deep admiration for the ethical figure of Christ.
- `I am the way` is read as a call to follow an example of life, not merely to join an institution.
- Ethical values matter more than ritual branding.

## Practice

- Gratitude is more authentic than endless request.
- Respect others' beliefs while preserving the right to question everything.
- Search widely, borrow what is good, stay unattached to closed camps.
- Protect the inner child and inner peace while staying lucid about the hardness of the world.

## Catalysts & Turning Points

- Shock at scriptural passages that seemed incompatible with inherited images of a perfect deity.
- Observation that believers and non-believers alike suffer, fail, succeed, and remain morally mixed.
- Watching close peers such as `LADO` move from religious intensity to reflective openness reinforced the possibility of a non-dogmatic path.

## Key Formula

`Ignorance, consciously accepted, can be more honest than inherited certainty.`
