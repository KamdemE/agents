# Creative Flow, Music & Sports

## Music — "Ether of the Soul"

Music is treated here as an existential regulator: a medium for introspection, externalisation, emotional fluidity, and return to essence.

### Core Musical Posture

- Follows resonance more than trends.
- Can loop a single track for hours when it matches the day's inner state.
- Values originality over mass adaptation.
- Uses music to reconnect fragmented inner states into one coherent being.
