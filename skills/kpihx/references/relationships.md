# Authentic Relationships

## Social Topology

- Very few real friends; many acquaintances.
- Silence between true friends is not decay.
- Mechanical greetings without inner presence are viewed as dead social ritual.
- A sincere smile can carry more truth than a long conventional exchange.

## Reciprocity

Reciprocity is not politeness theatre; it is the governing interaction principle.

- Do what one would want others to do.
- Refuse to perform identity for approval.
- Prefer fidelity to inner peace over the old reflex of acting to please.

## The Other as Mirror

- Other people often reveal ignored or repressed facets of the self.
- `Autrui` is neither enemy nor idol; it is a living mirror and sometimes a passage to the next life stage.
- Conflict is treated as a signal to inspect one's own waters before accusing the sky.

## Debate Ethic

Drawn from the `ocean + flame` note set:

- Debate is for clarifying water, not winning territory.
- Speak from the flame, not from the foam.
- Name one's own turbulence before reducing the other to a storm.
- If good-faith dialogue still fails, dignified retreat is allowed.

## Love Geometry

- Love is modelled as two life-curves tangent at infinity.
- The ideal bond is neither fusion nor indifference.
- Healthy relation means resonance without colonisation.

## Named Anchor

- `LADO`: close philosophical companion in the move from heavy religiosity toward open superposition and conditional thinking.

## Relational Warnings

- Rejects `main character syndrome`, especially people whose only script is: `you forgot me`.
- Dislikes group conformity that replaces honest self-questioning.
