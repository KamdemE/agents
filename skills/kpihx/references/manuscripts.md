This file is the sovereign note for the handwritten archive that documents KπX's intellectual development from childhood to `2024`, before the shift to primarily digital note-taking.

## Observed Patterns

- Long-range self-initiation rather than school-only execution.
- Recurrent urge to rebuild formulas and concepts from first principles.
- Early attraction to foundational anomalies: division by zero, `0!`, paradoxes, limiting processes, area/volume constants.
- Breadth across mathematics, computing, physics, ethics, and management.
- Strong continuity between scientific curiosity and explanatory/narrative instinct.
- Evidence that the later `ScienCurious Lab` posture was prefigured years earlier in raw handwritten form.

## Method Note

- Handwriting OCR is unreliable on this corpus.
- Preferred read path: render pages visually, inspect representative sequences, then anchor interpretations in concrete recurring motifs.
- Use `source_index.md` for the shortest exact source bundle when a claim must be challenged.

## Significance [INFERENCE]

The manuscript archive confirms that KπX's later positions are not post-hoc storytelling. The intuitive-formalist style, the desire to re-enact invention, the refusal of dead formal shells, the fascination with `pi` and reconstruction, and the crossover between technical and reflective writing were already active long before X or formal elite-track recognition.

The archive also supports a more precise reading of `seconde` as revelation rather than abrupt mutation: that year did not create the flame, it gave it tools, velocity, and conceptual keys.
